## What this site does not do

This is a static website. It has no user accounts, no comment section and no
forms. Specifically, it does **not**:

- set cookies of any kind
- use analytics, tracking pixels or advertising
- write to `localStorage` or `sessionStorage`
- load anything from a third party — no CDN, no external fonts, no embedded
  video or maps

Fonts are served from this domain rather than a font CDN, so rendering a page
does not disclose your IP address to a third party.

## Search

The search page works entirely in your browser. It downloads one static file of
page titles and text from this domain and filters it locally. Your search terms
are never transmitted anywhere.

## Server log files

Serving a web page requires your browser to connect to the hosting provider, and
that provider records technical data — typically IP address, time of request,
the file requested, and the referring page and user agent your browser sends.

This is necessary to deliver the site and to keep it secure, and is processed on
the basis of a legitimate interest, Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to request access to your personal data
(Art. 15), to have it corrected (Art. 16) or erased (Art. 17), to restrict its
processing (Art. 18), to receive it in a portable form (Art. 20), and to object
to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21).

To exercise any of these, contact the address above. You may also complain to a
supervisory authority in your country of residence.

## External links

Pages here link to sites we do not run, such as GitHub and npm. Following such a
link means the site you land on receives your request; this policy does not
apply there.
